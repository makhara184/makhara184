# Rasoi Atlas

## Open the app

[Launch Rasoi Atlas](https://makhara184.github.io/makhara184/apps/rasoi-atlas/) — use the live app in your browser. No GitHub account is needed.

The files listed above are the source code; use the launch link to cook with the app.

A browser-based Indian recipe atlas with a pantry matcher and ingredient alternatives. It is designed to grow beyond a short list of restaurant favourites by organising dishes across North, South, East, West, Central, and Northeast kitchens.

## What it does

- Search and filter recipes by dish, ingredient, region, state, diet, and cooking time.
- Enter what is in your kitchen; recipes are ranked by ingredients already available and missing essentials.
- Adjust servings, mark ingredients and cooking steps, save favourites, and print a recipe.
- Ask for an alternative beside any ingredient. Each suggestion describes its confidence, ratio, and effect on the dish.
- Filter vegetarian, vegan, and non-vegetarian dishes.

## Honest substitution guidance

A food substitution cannot be guaranteed to work in every recipe. The app distinguishes close swaps, approximate swaps, and ingredients without a dependable direct substitute. It never treats an ingredient that merely adds heat, acidity, or texture as equivalent to a culturally defining ingredient. It is not an allergy checker: verify packaging, ingredients, and cross-contact yourself.

## Research and recipe notes

The initial library is a carefully selected first edition, not a claim that every Indian dish has been catalogued. Indian cooking includes household and community variations, regional language names, seasonal ingredients, and oral traditions. The app keeps those limits visible and links readers to the references used for its regional coverage. Its home-kitchen instructions are written in original language and adapted for common kitchen equipment.

## Run

Open `index.html` in a modern browser or serve the folder over HTTPS. The app uses vanilla JavaScript and local storage; no account or backend is required. Favourites and pantry entries stay on the current browser.
